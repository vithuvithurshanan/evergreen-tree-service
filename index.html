About

Evergreen Tree Service

Evergreen Tree Service is a certified, locally owned arborist company dedicated to the health, safety, and beauty of the trees on your property. We believe every tree tells a story — and with the right care, that story can last for generations.

Our team brings professional expertise and a genuine love of trees to every job. Whether it's routine pruning, hazard removal, or emergency response, we work carefully, cleanly, and with full respect for your property and the natural world around it.

We serve residential and commercial clients across the region, offering honest assessments, transparent pricing, and work you can count on. No upsells, no surprises — just dependable tree care done right.

The Story of

Evergreen Tree Service

Evergreen was founded by James Hartley, an ISA Certified Arborist with over 20 years of experience in tree care, urban forestry, and hazard assessment across southern Ontario.

James started Evergreen with a simple belief: that great tree work comes from people who genuinely care about trees and the people who depend on them. Every member of our crew shares that belief, and it shows in the quality of our work.

We're proud to be a local, family-run business — one that treats your property as if it were our own.
